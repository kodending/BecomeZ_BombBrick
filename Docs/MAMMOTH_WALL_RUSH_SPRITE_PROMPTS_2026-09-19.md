# 쇄벽 돌진 전용 스프라이트 시트

내장 Imagegen 사용. 기존 코가손·설가손·아이언터스크·가이아손 이미지는 픽셀 밀도와 재질 참고다. 코드로 반복 배치하던 벽 조각을 제거하고, 재질별 벽 전체의 등장·파괴를 새로 그린 4×4/16프레임 PNG로 교체한다. 피해 수치는 변경하지 않는다.

## RockWall16

Use case: stylized-concept. Production transparent RGBA pixel-art animation SPRITE ATLAS for a Unity 2.5D monster autobattler. The reference mammoth is ONLY a style/pixel density/material palette reference; draw NO mammoth and NO other characters.
EXACTLY 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 equal square cells, row-major animation order, no gutters, labels, text or grid. Transparent alpha background throughout; no opaque checkerboard. ONE cohesive whole obstacle wall in every intact frame, NOT repeated narrow posts, fence slats, brick patterns, chains or thin lines. Single stout irregular substantial barricade, frontal three-quarter game viewpoint slightly from above, visible thickness/top/side facets, NOT a tall pole. Match the chunky outlined pixel clusters of the tiny reference creature: wall approximately 64x48 logical pixels, limited 8-12 shade palette, clean deliberate pixel steps. No smooth gradients, painting, photoreal surface noise, 3D render, glow blur or antialiasing. Wide squat silhouette with irregular top, width about 1.7 times height. Camera, wall identity, ground baseline and center locked through frames.
LAYOUT each cell: full wall in frame 4 occupies center 62% cell width and 42% cell height, bottom contacts ground at 72% cell height. Leave margins for energetic flying fragments; all animation fits its cell, no crossing borders.
ANIMATION:
row 1 frame 0 a few ground cracks/small material fragments beginning to rise; frame 1 lower quarter emerges; frame 2 half the wall emerges pushing up small chips; frame 3 nearly full wall raised.
row 2 frame 4 FULL INTACT WALL, proud heavy solid readable material form, no cracks that break silhouette yet; frame 5 IMPACT: center forcibly punched apart, jagged burst and first big separated chunks, impact pale spark small; frame 6 wall splits into 5-7 big angular chunks blasting sideways and forward, no intact columns; frame 7 chunks separate much farther, energetic wide explosive motion.
row 3 frames 8,9,10,11: independent tumbling angular fragments travel outward, drop under gravity, diminish, material-colored small pixel debris and brief low dust/appropriate material wisps. NO wall remains by frame 9. Visible perspective ground spread and a few airborne chunks.
row 4 frames 12,13,14,15: last small fragments and short dust wisps settle and disappear; 14 only a few fading specks, 15 virtually empty transparent cell.
Important: genuinely DRAW progressive poses, do not repeat a static drawing with opacity changes. No big circular outline/ring VFX.
SUBJECT MATERIAL: heavy natural brown-grey mountain rock barricade for shaggy brown mammoth. Broad irregular angular boulders fused as ONE rugged natural wall, thick ochre stone facets, charcoal brown outline, warm grey/brown midtones, sandy highlights. Large uneven broken silhouette, convincing weight. Shattering sprays chunky rock wedges and ochre ground dust.
